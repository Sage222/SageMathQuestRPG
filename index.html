Math Quest RPG

Long ago, the kingdom of Numeria was protected by the Ancient Formula — a spell of pure mathematics that kept the land safe and every creature in the realm calm and peaceful.

One stormy night, the Formula shattered. Monsters now roam the fields, forests, and mountains, and the old castles stand empty and guarded.

You are the last apprentice of Numeria's wisest wizard. Only someone who can solve the ancient number-riddles in battle can weaken the monsters, reclaim the lost treasure, and restore peace to the land.

Your quest begins now, hero.
